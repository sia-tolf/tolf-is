Why TOLF?

TOLF Network Suite brings together VPN, Smart DNS and configuration tools. Choose the tool you need and pay for the traffic you use with the VPN, without a monthly subscription.

Pay for gigabytes, not calendar days

Buy a traffic allowance instead of a month of access. This suits travel, occasional connections and specific tasks. Downloads and uploads both count; 1 GB means 1 billion bytes.

Keep your unused balance

Paid traffic has no expiry date. Unused gigabytes remain on your balance, and top-ups add to what is left. There is no need to use up a package before the end of the month.

No subscription or automatic charges

You decide when to top up and how much traffic to buy. Prices and available payment methods are shown before purchase. Buying traffic does not create a monthly subscription.

Try it on your own network first

A 1 GB trial is available once per registered account, without a bank card or time limit. Test the connection on your device and with your network provider. Trial and paid balances are shown separately; your account shows whether activation is available.

Choose your connection method

TOLF supports iPhone, iPad, Android and Windows. VPN options include IKEv2 and AnyConnect; iOS users can also use Shadowrocket. Available methods depend on the device. Shadowrocket is purchased separately. Quick setup helps you get started, while manual setup lets you choose parameters.

Let local sites use your regular connection

In a Shadowrocket configuration, you can choose direct access for Russian .ru, .su and .рф domains and Latvian .lv domains. These requests use your regular connection and IP address without consuming TOLF VPN traffic. These rules apply to this connection method and are not an automatic feature of every client.

Three tools for different tasks

VPN creates a tunnel for network traffic. Smart DNS encrypts DNS requests and applies rules to their handling, but does not replace a VPN or encrypt all device traffic. Smart DNS is included while your paid traffic balance is positive; access conditions are shown in your account. The free configurator creates VPN and DNS profiles from your settings and works without registration.

When TOLF fits, and when to compare

For occasional VPN use, a balance that does not expire lets you pay for actual traffic. If you transfer large amounts every day, compare your expected traffic cost with an unlimited subscription: paying per gigabyte may cost more. Speed and availability depend on your provider, network and selected server; the trial lets you test before buying.
